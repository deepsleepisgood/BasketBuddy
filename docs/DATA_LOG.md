# Data log

Every source, cleaning step and assumption behind BasketBuddy's data. The two
"generated" sections below are rewritten by the cleaning scripts on every run;
everything else is hand-written.

(archanaskitchen.com), originally published as *"6000+ Indian Food Recipes Dataset"*
on Mendeley Data, DOI [10.17632/xsphgmmh7b.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/xsphgmmh7b.1), CC BY 4.0.
The older Kaggle copy `kanishk307/6000-indian-food-recipes-dataset` has been removed (it
returned HTTP 403 on 11 Nov 2026), so it is not used.

All three Kaggle files were downloaded on **11 Nov 2026**; their row counts and
column names matched the expected schema exactly.

## Assumptions and known limitations

1. **Static price snapshot.** Prices and ratings come from a 2022 scrape. The bot
   never claims current prices, stock, availability or delivery times.
2. **No stock data.** Nothing in the dataset says whether a product is in stock or in which city.
3. **Pack size is mostly missing.** Only ~1% of product names state a size; the CSV
   has no size column. The same product appears at several prices with no size to
   tell them apart (e.g. Fortune *Sun Lite Sunflower Refined Oil* at ₹866 and ₹171,
   probably 5 L and 1 L). The bot lists every variant rather than guessing.
4. **Duplicate listings** (same product, brand and price under two sub-categories)
   are the same item, so one row is kept and the other sub-category stored in `also_in`.
5. **Missing ratings are unknown, not bad.** 31% of products have no rating; "Top
   rated" mode ranks them below rated products and never treats them as 0.
6. **Domain mapping is hand-made** (`map_domain` in `ingest/clean_products.py`), and
   so are the ingredient → category hints and the Hinglish synonym map used at retrieval time.
7. **Recipe servings may be missing or wrong.** Values outside 1–20 are treated as
   missing, and scaling is skipped for those recipes.
8. **Translated recipes are machine-translated** from Hindi on the source site; a
   few ingredient names are awkward ("chickpea lentils" for chana dal).
9. **Kits are synthetic.** Item lists in `kits.json` are written by the team as
   reasonable shopping lists and are labelled as such in the UI.
10. **AI-suggested lists are not data.** When no kit matches a need, the LLM proposes an item list. The UI
    labels it "AI-generated" and the user must confirm it before searching; the products and prices are still
    only BigBasket rows.

Other transformations (no rows removed):
- Recipes whose *instructions* were still Hindi but whose name and ingredients were English: kept, instructions set to empty (**266** rows). Instructions are never embedded.
- `name` = display name with the word "Recipe" / "(Recipe In Hindi)" removed, used for matching and dedup; the original is kept as `display_name`.
- Left-joined `Cleaned_Indian_Food_Dataset.csv` on URL (ignoring http/https and www, which differ between the files): **5,844 / 6,163 recipes matched (94.8%)**. Unmatched recipes fall back to regex/LLM extraction from `ingredients` at query time.
- Servings outside 1–20 treated as missing (**48** rows, e.g. 1000).
- Cuisine: stripped trailing " Recipes" and stray BOM characters; diet typo "Non Vegeterian" fixed.
- **Non-Indian cuisines kept** (1,966 recipes, flagged `is_indian=False`). Decision: shoppers do cook pasta, burritos and Thai curry, and those dishes still map to BigBasket products. Revisit only if they hurt dish-name retrieval in the eval.
